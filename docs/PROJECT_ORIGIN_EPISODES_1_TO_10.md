### Episode 01: "The Gilded Throne"
- **Global Timeline:** $t = 0.0\text{s} \text{ to } 60.0\text{s}$
- **Setting:** Silph-Macro Executive Spire (Floor 40), Saffron City. Night, heavy rain against glass.
- **Micro-Beats:**
  - **00:00 – 00:03 [The Hook]:** Close-up on a gold-embossed document sliding across a polished obsidian table. A corporate voice speaks: *"Sign the covenant, World Monarch. Or your reign ends tonight."*
  - **00:03 – 00:18 [The Stakes]:** CEO Vance Stirling stands before panoramic floor-to-ceiling windows. He displays the **Helix Neural Chip**—a sleek, pulsing violet micro-device. Ash, sitting across, feels Pikachu on his shoulder growl menacingly. Stirling explains: *"100% obedience. Zero hesitation. Uncapped battle power. The League belongs to the Consortium now."*
  - **00:18 – 00:42 [The Action]:** Ash slams his fists on the desk: *"My Pokémon aren't your machines!"* Executive Enforcers unholster stun rifles. Ash calls: *"Pikachu, full output—Thunderbolt!"* A blinding golden explosion shatters the 40th-floor tempered glass. Sirens wail.
  - **00:42 – 00:52 [The Twist]:** Ash and Pikachu leap out of the shattered window into the rainy void, using Iron Tail against the exterior steel scaffolding to brake their descent onto the lower rooftop.
  - **00:52 – 01:00 [The Cliffhanger]:** Ash lands in a crouch, soaked. Suddenly, four red laser sights lock onto his chest. Across the rooftop, four cyber-armored Houndoom emerge from the shadows, fangs dripping with sparks. Black cut with text: **"EPISODE 2 NEXT"**.

  - **00:52 – 01:00 [The Cliffhanger]:** The rain suddenly vaporizes into steam. A deep, seismic roar rattles the concrete. The sky ignites in crimson fire. A colossal winged shadow blots out the storm clouds. Black cut!

### Episode 03: "The Dragon from the Storm"

- **Setting:** Cerulean Cape Rooftop ruins. Steam and raging fire.
- **Micro-Beats:**

  - **00:03 – 00:18 [The Stakes]:** Enforcers scramble in panic. Through the thick plumes of steam walks a legendary beast: a towering, battle-hardened **Charizard**, its tail flame burning intense white-hot.
  - **00:18 – 00:42 [The Action]:** Charizard sweeps its wings (*Wing Attack*), generating gale-force wind that hurls three armored Houndoom off their feet.   - **00:42 – 00:52 [The Twist]:** Ash stares in pure disbelief: *"Wait... that cap... the trainer from Mt. Silver?!"* The silent trainer makes a single subtle hand signal. Charizard drops down beside Ash, extending a massive scaly claw.
  - **00:52 – 01:00 [The Cliffhanger]:** Red's voice speaks for the first time—deep, quiet, resonant: *"Get on. They track your Aura."* Ash grabs Pikachu and mounts Charizard. As they launch into the stratosphere, Kage glares upward, firing a tracker beacon. Black cut!

### Episode 04: "The Cabin at Mt. Silver"
- **Global Timeline:** $t = 180.0\text{s} \text{ to } 240.0\text{s}$
- **Setting:** Mt. Silver Peak, sub-zero blizzard $\rightarrow$ Fortified Log Cabin.
- **Micro-Beats:**

  - **00:03 – 00:18 [The Stakes]:** Red pushes open a heavy reinforced steel door into an underground cavern cabin warmed by a massive hearth fire. A giant Snorlax sleeps near the fire, radiating protective heat.
  - **00:18 – 00:42 [The Action]:** Ash demands answers: *"Who are you really? Why did they attack me? Leon, Cynthia... do the Champions know about this?!"* Red remains completely silent, brewing a pot of herbal medicine. He sets a cup before Ash, then pulls down a canvas wall tarp.
  - **00:42 – 00:52 [The Twist]:** Behind the tarp is a massive tactical intelligence map: satellite photos of 8 secret Silph-Macro bio-labs across Kanto and Johto, labeled with genetic codes: **Project Chimera, Genesis Core, Mew-0**.
  - **00:52 – 01:00 [The Cliffhanger]:** Red taps his finger on **Outpost 4** near Route 9: *"They didn't just build chips, Ash. They're harvesting the gods."* Outside the cabin, a red LED light on Charizard's rear talon begins blinking rapidly—the tracking beacon! Black cut!

  - **00:42 – 00:52 [The Twist]:** Ash doesn't retreat; he commands Pikachu to channel *Electroweb* through Snorlax's own body, using Snorlax's mass as an electrical ground. Snorlax grunts, pushed back 2 feet.
  - **00:52 – 01:00 [The Cliffhanger]:** Red's expression shifts—a faint, approving smirk. Suddenly, an explosive breach charge detonates the outer cavern entrance! Debris flies. Kage's voice echoes: *"Found you, ghosts."* Black cut!

  - **00:18 – 00:42 [The Action]:** Ash uses his Aura sensitivity, ducking under a swipe and rolling behind Ursaring. He calls: *"Pikachu, pinpoint precision—Iron Tail directly on the chip's mounting brackets, NOT the neck!"* Pikachu leaps, striking the metallic interface with surgical micro-force.

Ash holds the smoking microchip in his palm, breathing hard. Suddenly, the chamber's blast doors seal shut. An intercom crackles: Vance Stirling's voice. Black cut!
