# 🐾 PROJECT ORIGIN: THE SHATTERED CROWN
## Master Pokémon Roster, Movesets & Combat Mechanics (Arc 1: Ep 1–10)

This registry establishes canonical stats, combat roles, move-pools, visual aura signatures, and audio effects for all Pokémon appearing in Arc 1.

---

### 1. Protagonist Roster

#### Ash's Pikachu
- **Classification:** Mouse Pokémon | **Typing:** Electric
- **Battle Role:** High-Velocity Infiltrator & Kinetic Pivot
- **Move-pool in Arc 1:**
  1. *Thunderbolt:* 100,000V golden arc; can be modulated from narrow surgical piercing rays to wide-angle dome barriers.
  2. *Iron Tail:* Tail hardens into metallic chrome with high kinetic impact; used defensively to parry blades and brake falls.
  3. *Electro Web:* Electrified plasma netting; used for crowd containment, wall rebounding, and non-lethal disarms.
  4. *Quick Attack:* Blurring white contrails; breaks optical tracking systems of automated drones.
- **Audio Sound Design:** High-voltage sharp crackle ($2.5\text{ kHz}$ to $8\text{ kHz}$ hiss and snap), energetic battle cry.

#### Red's Charizard
- **Classification:** Flame Pokémon | **Typing:** Fire / Flying (Fire / Dragon in Mega X)
- **Battle Role:** Heavy Aerial Dreadnought & Shock Weapon
- **Scale:** 2.2 meters tall (significantly larger than standard $1.7\text{m}$ dex entry).
- **Move-pool in Arc 1:**
  1. *Blast Burn:* Superheated blue-white seismic blast that craters reinforced concrete.
  2. *Dragon Claw:* Emerald-green energy blades extending from claws; cuts through armored hull plating.
  3. *Flame Charge:* Envelopes entire body in a supersonic thermal vortex; used for aerial interception.
  4. *Wing Attack:* Creates localized hurricane-force pressure waves to deflect projectile volleys.
- **Audio Sound Design:** Deep guttural draconic roar mixed with jet engine turbine spool-up, heavy bass concussions ($60\text{ Hz}$).

#### Red's Snorlax
- **Classification:** Sleeping Pokémon | **Typing:** Normal
- **Battle Role:** Kinetic Redirection & Immovable Bastion
- **Move-pool in Arc 1:**
  1. *Heavy Slam:* Leverages its $1,000+\text{ lbs}$ mass to generate localized earth tremors.
  2. *Rest / Sleep Talk:* Instant cellular regenerative recovery under battlefield conditions.
  3. *Earthquake:* Subterranean seismic fracture used to disable mechanized wheels and treads.
- **Combat Style:** Does not dodge; absorbs kinetic energy into dense fatty tissue and redirects force back at attackers.

#### Red's Venusaur
- **Classification:** Seed Pokémon | **Typing:** Grass / Poison
- **Battle Role:** Crowd Suppression & Battlefield Architecture
- **Move-pool in Arc 1:**
  1. *Frenzy Plant:* Massive iron-hard tree roots erupt from subterranean rock to dismantle structures.
  2. *Sleep Powder:* High-density bioluminescent spore cloud; induces rapid non-lethal sedation.
  3. *Vine Whip:* High-tensile steel-like vine tendrils used for precision disarms and structural grappling.

### 2. Antagonist & Chipped Pokémon Roster (Episodes 1–10)

#### Consortium Cyber-Houndoom Squad (Unit 4)
- **Typing:** Dark / Fire
- **Modifications:** Carbon-composite skull armor, synthetic jaw actuators, violet **Helix Chip v1.0** bolted to the spinal column.
- **Tactical Behavior:** Pack hunting, pack flanking, zero fear response, ignores minor limb injuries.
- **Moveset:** *Flamethrower* (chemical orange-violet flame), *Crunch*, *Thunder Fang*, *Sludge Bomb*.
- **VFX Signature:** Unblinking dilated red pupils, intermittent purple spark discharges along spine.

#### Chipped Heavy Steelix (Commander Kage's Vanguard)
- **Typing:** Steel / Ground
- **Modifications:** Reinforced diamond-tipped jaw drill, hydraulic servo-collars along steel body rings.
- **Moveset:** *Iron Tail*, *Stone Edge*, *Autotomize* (causes the massive metallic snake to glide at terrifying speed), *Heavy Slam*.

#### Subject 08: The Berserker Ursaring (Route 9 Outpost 4)
- **Typing:** Normal
- **Condition:** Overclocked adrenal synthesis via prototype high-voltage Helix Chip.
- **Height & Mass:** 2.8 meters | $380\text{ kg}$
- **Moveset:**
  1. *Hyper Voice:* Acoustic shockwave that shatters reinforced laboratory glassware and destabilizes inner ear balance.
  2. *Slash:* Razor-sharp claws leaving 4-inch deep gouges in steel support girders.
  3. *Hammer Arm:* Devastating downward bludgeoning attack capable of crushing hovercraft hulls.
- **Resolution:** Chip surgically disconnected by Ash's Pikachu (*Iron Tail* on bracket) and sedated by Red's Venusaur (*Sleep Powder*).

### 3. The Helix Chip Mechanics & Visual Language

```
[Silph Master Server] 
         │ (Encrypted 5.8 GHz Telemetry)
         ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ Helix Chip v1.0                 │
│ • Shuts down pain receptors     │ ──► [Target Pokémon]
│ • Forces 400% adrenaline surge  │     • Violet glowing iris
│ • Bypasses natural exhaustion   │     • Blackened vascular lines
└─────────────────────────────────┘     • Zero fatigue response
```

- **Visual Manifestation:** A geometric hexagonal violet titanium module ($5\text{cm} \times 3\text{cm}$) with dual micro-needles piercing the cervical spine. When active, glowing ultraviolet vein-like lines branch through the surrounding muscle and fur.
- **Vulnerability:** Disconnecting the chip immediately causes the target to collapse into deep biological recovery sleep (saving the Pokémon without needing to harm it).
