# 👥 PROJECT ORIGIN: THE SHATTERED CROWN
## Master Character Design Sheets & Generation Tokens

This specification contains character visual prompt tokens (for AI image/video synthesis), English voice acting direction, psychological profiles, and combat rules.

---

### 1. Ash Ketchum (The Reigning World Monarch)
- **Age:** 16  
- **Title:** Reigning World Monarch (Masters Eight Champion)  
- **Aesthetic Philosophy:** Functional Shonen Champion — athletic, battle-tested, unpretentious.

#### Visual Prompt Tokens (Dense Keyframe Injection)
> `16-year-old anime hero Ash Ketchum, slightly matured athletic build, spiky messy black hair beneath an unbuttoned World Monarch ceremonial red-and-white trainer cap. Wearing a dark navy blue athletic compression undershirt, unbuttoned sleeveless royal-blue vest with white collar and gold trim. Fingerless black leather training gloves; right wrist wears an Alolan Z-Ring with Pikashunium Z, left wrist wears a Mega Evolution keystone bracelet. Expressive determined brown eyes, subtle zig-zag cheek marks. Confident battle stance, intense kinetic energy.`

#### Psychological Profile
- **Core Motivation:** Protection of Pokémon autonomy. To Ash, Pokémon are family and partners, never tools or biological weapons.
- **Flaws & Vulnerabilities:** Idealistic, struggles with the moral compromises of corporate power, feels personal guilt when Pokémon under his watch are harmed.
- **Evolution across Arc 1:** Forced to grow beyond stadium rulebooks. Learns to operate in real-world combat where opponents do not wait for referee whistles.

#### Voice & Dialogue Direction (Cinematic English Dub)
- **Pitch & Timbre:** Mid-to-high tenor, youthful grit, energetic, resolute.
- **Speech Habits:** Shouts commands with deep emotional resonance (*"Pikachu, let's show them what a true bond looks like!"*). Speaks directly from the heart, never uses corporate jargon.
- **Reference Voice Model:** Anime dub protagonist cadence (energetic, earnest, passionate).

### 2. Red (The Phantom of Mt. Silver)
- **Age:** 19  
- **Title:** The Living Myth / Former Kanto Champion  
- **Aesthetic Philosophy:** Gritty Tactical Hermit — weathered, disciplined, silent lethality.

#### Visual Prompt Tokens (Dense Keyframe Injection)
> `19-year-old legendary trainer Red, tall lean muscular build, dark shaggy hair partially concealed beneath a worn, faded red-and-white cap casting a deep dramatic shadow over the upper half of his face. Piercing, intense crimson-tinted eyes glowing in the shadow. Wearing a high-collared charcoal-black duster trenchcoat with deep crimson interior lining, dark tactical cargo pants, sturdy combat boots, fingerless combat gloves with wrapped wrists. Stoic, unreadable facial expression, imposing aura of extreme battle mastery.`

#### Psychological Profile
- **Core Motivation:** Absolute vigilance against threats to Pokémon species survival. Left society after realizing the League was compromised by corporate interests.
- **Flaws & Vulnerabilities:** Emotionally distant, borderline nihilistic about human institutions, relies solely on himself and his six partner Pokémon.
- **Evolution across Arc 1:** Observing Ash’s stubborn refusal to abandon captured wild Pokémon re-ignites Red’s faith in human-Pokémon bonds.

#### Voice & Dialogue Direction (Cinematic English Dub)
- **Pitch & Timbre:** Low bass-baritone, gravelly, quiet, chillingly calm.
- **Speech Habits:** Uses fewer than 10 words per episode. Communicates primarily through single-word commands (*"Spread."*, *"Down."*, *"Burn."*, *"Clear."*). When he speaks in full sentences, the entire room falls silent.
- **Reference Voice Model:** Deep, stoic cinematic veteran cadence.

### 3. CEO Vance Stirling (Chairman of the Silph-Macro Consortium)
- **Age:** 48  
- **Title:** Chairman & Chief Executive Officer  
- **Aesthetic Philosophy:** Corporate Transhumanist Oligarch.

#### Visual Prompt Tokens (Dense Keyframe Injection)
> `48-year-old ruthless corporate executive Vance Stirling, slicked-back silver-gray hair, sharp aristocratic cheekbones, cold ice-blue eyes behind thin titanium-rimmed glasses. Wearing an impeccably tailored charcoal-gray three-piece pinstripe suit, burgundy silk tie, gold lapel pin in the shape of a stylized DNA helix. Elegant, composed, sociopathic posture, holding an encrypted violet holographic datapad.`

#### Psychological Profile
- **Core Motivation:** Total monopolization of Pokémon evolution and combat through cybernetic telemetry. Believes wild Pokémon are unpredictable biological hazards that must be mechanized for human safety and profit.
- **Speech Style:** Smooth, articulate, chillingly polite. Treats high-stakes cruelty as mere quarterly business decisions (*"A shame, Ash. Your stock had such promising returns."*).

### 6. Signature Partner Pokémon Profiles

#### Ash's Pikachu
- **Visual Signatures:** Brown stripe pattern, slight notch on right ear from previous historic battles, cheeks sparking bright golden electricity.
- **Personality:** Incorruptible, fiercely protective of Ash, playful during quiet moments but razor-sharp in combat.

#### Red's Charizard
- **Visual Signatures:** 1.5x larger than standard Charizard, jagged scar across left wing membrane, tail flame burns blue-white at base (indicating intense thermal core output), primal ferocious gaze.
- **Battle Relationship:** Reads Red’s micro-gestures instantly. Can Mega Evolve into Mega Charizard X without verbal command via emotional resonance.

#### Red's Snorlax
- **Visual Signatures:** Massive, weathered fur with moss-tinted scuffs from Mt. Silver snowdrifts. Closes eyes in apparent slumber while tracking ground vibrations with sub-millimeter precision.
- **Role:** The Immovable Bastion. Intercepts ballistic attacks with body weight and defensive mass.

#### Subject 08 (The Berserker Ursaring)
- **Visual Signatures:** Huge 8-foot Ursaring, fur stained with industrial coolant, pulsing jagged violet Helix Chip bolted directly into the cervical vertebrae behind its neck. Veins glowing radioactive purple through muscle tissue.
